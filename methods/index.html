survey definitions

Preserve what the survey asked.

A variable name does not fully define a measurement. The question, respondent instructions, population covered, response codes and survey vintage determine what an observed amount means. The construction pipeline needs those definitions alongside the data.

The 2024 ACS rent item illustrates this. The paper questionnaire, item 18a, asks for monthly rent. The 2024 Subject Definitions, printed page 16, adds: “The respondent was to exclude subsidies paid by a local housing authority or other agency.” It also includes some payments by people or organizations outside the household. This is the publisher's description of its instructions; the question alone does not establish the subsidy exclusion.

That distinction affects a model that starts with rent before assistance and subtracts a modeled subsidy. Mapping the ACS reported amount directly to that input can subtract assistance twice. The reported amount, measured assistance status and the model's charge before assistance need separate definitions. The new US construction work has not yet established the final mapping or evaluated its national and district effects.

A local implementation of Chronicle, our source-evidence registry, preserves the original public PDFs, exact extracted page text and checked byte spans for six excerpts from three Census documents. An independent consumer reopens each PDF and reproduces the extraction. The questionnaire, subject definition and a 2019 methodology paper's historical account of interview instructions remain separate, dated claims. These artifacts have not been registered or witnessed as a Chronicle release.

SIPP periods and missing values

The 2023 Survey of Income and Program Participation (SIPP) mostly describes 2022, but its tax questions concern filing for tax year 2021. The User Guide, printed page 111, footnote 14, makes that distinction explicit. The filing question and its calendar-year fill are preserved separately. A question-shaped API label is dictionary metadata; it is not a substitute for the instrument's verbatim text.

The dependent-claim question is restricted to people aged 15–25 in December whose filing status was single. A blank outside that published question universe cannot become a “no.” The suppression rules likewise explain why some entire columns are missing. The seventh-job monthly earnings field is suppressed; a blank in it does not establish zero earnings.

SIPP also distinguishes reported values from several forms of imputation. Status flag 9 directs the reader to a recode's component flags. It does not itself mean “as reported.” The model's treatment of a published value and the evidence about how that value was produced need separate records.

evidence identity

Bind the claim to the population.

A comparison needs the release identifier and population digest, period and entity, model version, target or reference snapshot, and geographic definition. The population build run and the evaluation run can differ. Matching a file's digest verifies its bytes; an evaluation protocol determines what those bytes support.

Calibration fit measures agreement with facts used to choose weights. A related fact from the same source can share measurement error or information with those targets. Held-out evidence needs a recorded exclusion from fitting and a stated relationship to the training sources. A model's validation-reach inventory describes checks on that model; it does not automatically describe a selected population.

The candidate-evidence work keeps execution status separate from release eligibility. Missing attachments remain missing. geographic scope

A district label needs a boundary vintage.

Record the source geography, its vintage, the destination boundaries and any crosswalk separately. Allocating a source district's total across later boundaries creates a modeled geographic transformation. It does not turn the original observation into a direct measurement of the destination district.

National agreement can coexist with district errors. A national reference row has to be acquired directly; summing district rows produces a derived quantity rather than a published national reference, and a holdout drawn from the same source as the fitting targets is not independent-source evidence. For each intended analysis area, inspect weighted distributions, missingness, record support and weight concentration alongside target fit and held-out comparisons. Effective sample size describes weight concentration; it does not measure bias or establish independent source coverage.

Existing local-area releases retain the scope and limitations declared in their own release records. The new build's acceptance work does not retrospectively certify or invalidate those releases. A compact member of a population family needs its own geographic evidence; it does not inherit district readiness from a larger member.

District estimates can describe incidence and distributional shape within their stated scope. A district label alone does not establish fiscal-note-grade district revenue accuracy. Compare candidate and incumbent only when periods, entities, weight definitions, bins and geography vintages align.

data access and privacy

Provenance and access controls serve different purposes.

Source confidentiality and redistribution rights follow the data's origin and permitted derivations. Storage gating controls who can download a file. An authenticated repository can contain an openly redistributable artifact; a public endpoint does not make confidential source material releasable. The release contract records storage visibility and source-licence restrictions separately.

Synthetic records can preserve observed attributes, copy donor values or contain fitted draws. A deterministic transformation or fixed random seed preserves reproducibility, not a privacy guarantee. Imputation and reweighting do not erase confidential source ancestry.

The design charter restricts proposed private-source contributions to certified conditional models and calls for disclosure checks, formal privacy certificates and composition of privacy budgets. It describes private-source attestation and audit infrastructure as unfinished. A fitted model alone does not satisfy those requirements.

This site makes no blanket differential-privacy or non-disclosure guarantee for synthetic data. Each release needs its own source, licence, derivation and disclosure statement. Public diagnostics should expose aggregate evidence within those release boundaries.
